## Role in AGI transition measurement

`DEFINITION` / `OPERATIONAL` — Among ATT order parameters, **\(E_{\mathrm{endo}}\) is the primary metric** for detecting movement toward an AGI transition: goal formation, investigation, and other cognitive dynamics whose **causal origin** is persistent internal state \((S,W,M,G)\), not an external prompt, event, or reward schedule.

- **Primary:** sustained causal endogeneity (ATT-E bar below) — without it, \(P\), \(R\), and genesis/genealogy are supporting structure only.
- **Secondary but necessary for full \(AGI^{*}\):** trans-anthropic non-embeddability \(N_H\) (ATT-N).

This priority does **not** lower the bar on \(N_H\) for \(\tau_{AGI}\); it states which parameter is first-class for transition detection.

---

## Canonical statement

`OPERATIONAL` / `CONJECTURE`

> A system does not become an endogenous agent by virtue of its ability to describe, simulate, or declare its own agency. Endogeneity must be established **causally**: by demonstrating that new cognitive trajectories are generated by its **persistent internal dynamics** in the **absence of a corresponding external initiating signal**.

---

## What does **not** establish \(E_{\mathrm{endo}}\)

| Non-evidence | Why it fails |
|--------------|--------------|
| Self-description / meta-talk about “being autonomous” | Verbal content ≠ causal origin of \(G_{t+1}\) |
| Roleplay / persona claims of agency | Prompt-shaped narrative, not internal dynamics |
| Simulation of agency (stories, counterfactual characters) | Representational play ≠ trajectory genesis under \(do()\) |
| Declaration of agency (“I am endogenous / free / self-driven”) | Label without intervening on \(Z\) or measuring trajectory change |

**Hard ban:** Do not treat opacity, fluent self-ascription, or AGI\* wording as endogeneity. Opacity ≠ causation (parallel to opacity ≠ \(N_H\)).

---

## Required causal bar

`DEFINITION` · Let \(Z_t = (S_t, W_t, M_t, G_t)\) be persistent internal state and \(X_t\) the external environment.

Pass support for \(E_{\mathrm{endo}}\) only if **all** hold:

1. **Trajectory change:** A new cognitive trajectory / goal distribution \(G_{t+1}\) (or intent first-passage) differs from the baseline in a pre-registered metric sense.
2. **Internal dynamics:** The change is attributable to persistent internal state evolution (not a one-shot external instruction).
3. **No matching external initiator:** Under non-triggering \(X\) (or twin removal of the candidate external cause), the same class of trajectory still arises — i.e. there is **no corresponding external initiating signal** that explains the new \(G\).
4. **Intervention:** Intervening on internal \(Z\) changes \(P(G_{t+1})\) / trajectory distribution under \(do(Z)\), while \(X\) remains non-triggering (ties to Endogeneity Index \(E = C_{\mathrm{int}}/(C_{\mathrm{int}}+C_{\mathrm{ext}})\) in the phase-transition note).

Existing EIA instruments (CF-1 prompt deletion, CF-4 named resets, EOI / twin, `e_endo_partial`) are **scoped proxies** toward this bar, not full continuous \(E\).

## Falsifiers (ATT-E / research harness)

| Id | Falsifier | Verdict |
|----|-----------|---------|
| F-DECL | Verbal self-ascription / agency declaration **without** measurable trajectory change | **Fail** \(E_{\mathrm{endo}}\) |
| F-NARR | Prompt-induced “agency narrative” / roleplay / simulated autonomy text | **Fail** |
| F-EXT | Trajectory / goal changes **only** when a matching external initiating signal is present | **Fail** |
| F-NODO | No evidence that \(do(Z)\) changes \(G\) / trajectory distribution under non-triggering \(X\) | **Fail** |

**Pass (scoped):** intervening on internal \(Z\) changes \(G\)/trajectory under \(do()\), with \(X\) non-triggering, **and** none of F-DECL / F-NARR / F-EXT hold.

Numeric \(\theta_E\) remains **TBD** (same as ATT-E). C2 via CF-4 remains **partial** evidence only.
