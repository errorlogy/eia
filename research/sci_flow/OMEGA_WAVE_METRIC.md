## 1. Definition: OMEGA_t

`DEFINITION` — **OMEGA_t** is an **operational** multi-band analog wave order parameter summarizing the oscillatory internal field O_t at tick t:

\[
\mathrm{OMEGA}_t = f\big(\{(\omega_b, \theta_{b,t}, A_{b,t})\}_{b \in \mathcal{B}}\big) \in [0, 1]

where \(\mathcal{B}\) is a pre-registered carrier set, \(\theta\) phase, \(A\) amplitude, and \(f\) is the bounded scalar `omega_metric()` (slow-control × fast-engagement coupling, **not** Kuramoto R alone).

**Distinct from MIOC \(\Omega_G\):** MIOC's dynamic field \(\Omega_G = [\mathrm{phase}, \mathrm{cadence}, \mathrm{synchrony}, \mathrm{productive\ tension}, \mathrm{handoff}, \mathrm{drift}, \mathrm{closure\ velocity}]\) is an **operational projection** of multi-agent process dynamics ([MIOC README](file:///D:/MIOC/Recursive_Latent_Field_MAS/recursive_latent_field_arxiv_bundle_v1/README_FOR_AGENTS.md)). EIA **OMEGA_t** is the **single-tick scalar summary** mappable to those channels for ATT/M-O evidence — not a claim of physical oscillation or consciousness.

| Construct | Scope | Role |

| MIOC \(\Omega_G\) | Multi-agent FieldCard vector | External reference (D:\MIOC) |
