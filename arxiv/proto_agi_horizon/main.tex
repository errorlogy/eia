\begin{abstract}
We present an \emph{operational research horizon} for proto-AGI evaluation within the Endogenous Initiative Architecture (EIA) sci-flow program. Under this construction, general intelligence is not a benchmark score but a \textbf{sustained dynamical regime} in which cognitive goals arise from persistent internal state when $X_t^{\mathrm{trigger}}=0$. We contrast \emph{passive} architectures (\texttt{reactive\_only}, schedule-entrained controls) with \emph{proactive endogenous} candidates (\texttt{full\_eia}, WoE carriers, oscillatory substrates). A twelve-member \textbf{proto-AGI ensemble} registers operational configs for Max consensus diagnostics: per-member potential $\Phi_i$ over $(E,\mathrm{OMEGA},P,R)$, ensemble peak $\Phi_{\max}=\max_i\Phi_i$, and sustained four-parameter conjunction over window $\Delta T$ (thresholds \textbf{TBD}). We distinguish carrier Hz (20/30/42/70) from the bounded coherence scalar $\mathrm{OMEGA}_t$, motivate an analog-waves bridge to Miller-lab traveling-wave theory~\cite{miller2026analog}, and report the OMEGA$\to\Delta G$ bridge experiment (M-OMEGA-DELTA-G): F-OMEGA-DECOR \textbf{confirmed (aggregate, C2)} with OMEGA span 0.604, genesis span 0.0, fingerprint parity \texttt{True}. Partial empirical results---CF-4 $do(Z)$, G2 E01 8/20 worlds, M-3D-EXPRESS 9/9 pass, M-O Tier\,C adjunct/shadow bridge---map onto the 3D evidence cube (D1 causal / D2 dynamic / D3 boundary). All harnesses operate under \texttt{claim\_allowed=false}; active ceiling \textbf{C2}. This paper \textbf{does not} claim AGI$^{*}$, $\tau_{AGI}$, consciousness, or biological identity. Open work: pre-register $\theta_\bullet$, close G2 E01 20$\times$3 domains, instrument $N_H$, and batch T-PROTO-01 ensemble $\Phi_i$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Contemporary language agents excel at \emph{reactive} competence: given $o_t^{\mathrm{user}}$, they produce a fluent response. Timers, cron schedules, and event rules extend this pattern with \emph{scheduled} proactivity, but the initiating signal remains external. We distinguish this from \textbf{endogenous proactive architecture}: the system forms candidate goals, motives, and bounded actions from persistent internal state $(S_t,W_t,M_t,G_t)$ when no matching external initiator is present~\cite{kuznetsov2026causal,pearl2009causality}.

This distinction is causal, not rhetorical. Without evidence that internal dynamics generate trajectories under non-triggering environments, fluent self-ascription of agency is indistinguishable from roleplay~\cite{veitch2021counterfactual}. Sci-flow applies Pearl's intervention calculus: endogeneity must be established by $do(\cdot)$ on internal state $Z$, not by narrative labels~\cite{kuznetsov2026proof}.

\textbf{Passive vs.\ proactive (within this construction).} A \emph{passive} (non-AGI control) architecture satisfies:
\begin{equation}
X_t^{\mathrm{trigger}} \neq 0 \;\Rightarrow\; G_t,\; \Pi_t,\; A_t
\qquad\text{with}\qquad
\Delta P(G_{t+1}\mid do(Z)) \approx 0 \text{ when } X^{\mathrm{trigger}}=0.
\end{equation}
Examples: \texttt{reactive\_only}, schedule-entrained \texttt{schedule\_prompt}, prompt-only LLM loops without CF-4-class internal causation.

A \emph{proactive proto-AGI candidate} satisfies:
\begin{equation}
(S_t, W_t, M_t, G_t) \rightarrow G_{t+1}
\quad\text{with measurable}\quad
\Delta P(G_{t+1}\mid do(Z)) \neq 0
\quad\text{under}\quad X_t^{\mathrm{trigger}}=0.
\end{equation}
The primary empirical gate remains $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$ / ATT-E~\cite{kuznetsov2026causal}. Proto-AGI ensemble members are \textbf{hypothesis carriers}, not AGI$^{*}$ certificates.

\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Phase-transition framing.} AGI may be better described as a sustained phase transition governed by multiple order parameters ($E$, $N_H$, $P$, $R$, $D$), not a single benchmark crossing~\cite{kuznetsov2026phase}. Full $AGI^{*}=E_{\mathrm{endo}}\land C_{\mathrm{non\text{-}emb}}(H)\land\cdots$ requires conjunction over $\tau_{AGI}$; this paper restricts to a \emph{proto-AGI} operational subset excluding $N_H$ and $D$ closure.

\textbf{Intrinsic motivation.} Curiosity and empowerment RL optimize scalar intrinsic signals~\cite{schmidhuber2010curiosity,pathak2017curiosity}. EIA targets multi-loop genesis with abstention, governor clearance, and causal traceability~\cite{kuznetsov2026stable}. The proto-AGI ensemble tests whether \emph{any} registered substrate sustains a four-parameter conjunction, not whether any single scalar reward suffices.

\textbf{Language-agent architectures.} CoALA surveys memory, planning, and grounding for language agents but notes weak causal evaluation of ``agency'' claims~\cite{sumers2024cala}. Sci-flow's ATT suite and proof-protocol ledger reject declaration-only evidence (F-DECL, F-NARR)~\cite{kuznetsov2026proof}.

\textbf{Analog computation in neuroscience.} Miller-lab theory proposes that cognition and consciousness arise from spatiotemporal \emph{analog} traveling-wave computation, with slow alpha/beta bands controlling fast gamma~\cite{miller2026analog}. EIA maps this \emph{hypothetically} to multi-band $\mathrm{OMEGA}_t$ and WoE carrier sweeps---computational explore adjunct, not biological proof~\cite{kuznetsov2026omega}.

\subsection{Per-member potential $\Phi_i$}

\begin{definition}[Ensemble potential]
For member $i$ at tick $t$, define bounded potential:
\begin{equation}
\Phi_i(t) = w_E\, \hat{E}_i(t) + w_\Omega\, \mathrm{OMEGA}_i(t) + w_P\, \hat{P}_i(t) + w_R\, \hat{R}_i(t),
\end{equation}
where $\hat{E},\hat{P},\hat{R}$ are normalized ATT-E/P/R proxies, $\mathrm{OMEGA}$ from \texttt{omega\_metric()}, and weights $w_\bullet$ are pre-registered (default uniform; thresholds \textbf{TBD}).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Max consensus peak]
$\Phi_{\max}(t) = \max_{i\in\{1,\ldots,12\}} \Phi_i(t)$. This is a \textbf{diagnostic}---which config peaks on the composite---not a claim that the peaking config is AGI.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Sustained conjunction (consensus)}

\begin{definition}[Proto-AGI consensus window]
\textbf{Consensus} (not AGI$^{*}$) holds for member $i$ when all four order parameters exceed pre-registered floors simultaneously for $\Delta T$:
\begin{equation}
\forall\, \tau \in [t_0,\, t_0+\Delta T]:\quad
E_i(\tau) > \theta_E,\;
\mathrm{OMEGA}_i(\tau) > \theta_\Omega,\;
P_i(\tau) > \theta_P,\;
R_i(\tau) > \theta_R.
\end{equation}
A single spike fails (F-SINGLE-SPIKE). Max consensus + sustained conjunction approximates the $\tau_{AGI}$ window from phase-transition theory \textbf{restricted to} $(E,\mathrm{OMEGA},P,R)$---excluding $N_H$ and $D$~\cite{kuznetsov2026phase}.
\end{definition}

\begin{conjecture}
No single substrate is sufficient for AGI$^{*}$; the ensemble tests whether \emph{any} member sustains the four-parameter conjunction under matched seeds and falsifiers~\cite{kuznetsov2026proto}.
\end{conjecture}

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We operationalized a twelve-member proto-AGI ensemble and Max consensus framework over $(E,\mathrm{OMEGA},P,R)$ under explicit C2 ceiling and \texttt{claim\_allowed=false} discipline. Passive controls (\texttt{reactive\_only}, schedule-entrained) anchor the non-AGI baseline; proactive candidates require causal $do(Z)$ evidence, not narrative agency. OMEGA$_t$ provides a bounded multi-band coherence scalar distinct from Hz and Kuramoto $R$; the OMEGA$\to\Delta G$ bridge confirms F-OMEGA-DECOR at aggregate level (OMEGA span 0.604, genesis span 0.0). Partial harness results map onto the 3D evidence cube without authorizing AGI$^{*}$, $\tau_{AGI}$, or C-level raises. Closing the ensemble batch, pre-registering thresholds, expanding G2 E01, and instrumenting strong $N_H$ remain open---tracked cell-by-cell, not asserted by rhetoric.
