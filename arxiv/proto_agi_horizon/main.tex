\begin{abstract}
We present an \emph{operational research horizon} for proto-AGI evaluation within the Endogenous Initiative Architecture (EIA)~\cite{kuznetsov2026eia} sci-flow program. Under this construction, general intelligence is not a benchmark score but a \textbf{sustained dynamical regime} in which cognitive goals arise from persistent internal state when $X_t^{\mathrm{trigger}}=0$. We contrast \emph{passive} architectures (\texttt{reactive\_only}, schedule-entrained controls) with \emph{proactive endogenous} candidates (\texttt{full\_eia}, WoE carriers, oscillatory substrates). A twelve-member \textbf{proto-AGI ensemble} registers operational configs for Max consensus diagnostics: per-member potential $\Phi_i$ over $(E,\mathrm{OMEGA},P,R)$, ensemble peak $\Phi_{\max}=\max_i\Phi_i$, and sustained four-parameter conjunction over window $\Delta T$ (thresholds \textbf{TBD}). All harnesses operate under \texttt{claim\_allowed=false}; active ceiling \textbf{C2}. This paper \textbf{does not} claim AGI$^{*}$, $\tau_{AGI}$, consciousness, or biological identity. Open work: pre-register $\theta_\bullet$, close G2 E01 20$\times$3 domains, instrument $N_H$, and batch T-PROTO-01 ensemble $\Phi_i$.

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Contemporary language agents excel at \emph{reactive} competence: given $o_t^{\mathrm{user}}$, they produce a fluent response. Timers, cron schedules, and event rules extend this pattern with \emph{scheduled} proactivity, but the initiating signal remains external. We distinguish this from \textbf{endogenous proactive architecture}: the system forms candidate goals, motives, and bounded actions from persistent internal state $(S_t,W_t,M_t,G_t)$ when no matching external initiator is present~\cite{kuznetsov2026causal,pearl2009causality}.

This distinction is causal, not rhetorical. Without evidence that internal dynamics generate trajectories under non-triggering environments, fluent self-ascription of agency is indistinguishable from roleplay~\cite{veitch2021counterfactual}. Sci-flow applies Pearl's intervention calculus: endogeneity must be established by $do(\cdot)$ on internal state $Z$, not by narrative labels~\cite{kuznetsov2026proof}.

\textbf{Passive vs.\ proactive (within this construction).} A \emph{passive} (non-AGI control) architecture satisfies:
\begin{equation}
X_t^{\mathrm{trigger}} \neq 0 \;\Rightarrow\; G_t,\; \Pi_t,\; A_t
\qquad\text{with}\qquad
\Delta P(G_{t+1}\mid do(Z)) \approx 0 \text{ when } X^{\mathrm{trigger}}=0.
\end{equation}
Examples: \texttt{reactive\_only}, schedule-entrained \texttt{schedule\_prompt}, prompt-only LLM loops without CF-4-class internal causation.

A \emph{proactive proto-AGI candidate} satisfies:
\begin{equation}
(S_t, W_t, M_t, G_t) \rightarrow G_{t+1}
\quad\text{with measurable}\quad
\Delta P(G_{t+1}\mid do(Z)) \neq 0
\quad\text{under}\quad X_t^{\mathrm{trigger}}=0.
\end{equation}
The primary empirical gate remains $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$ / ATT-E~\cite{kuznetsov2026causal}. Proto-AGI ensemble members are \textbf{hypothesis carriers}, not AGI$^{*}$ certificates.

\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Phase-transition framing.} AGI may be better described as a sustained phase transition governed by multiple order parameters ($E$, $N_H$, $P$, $R$, $D$), not a single benchmark crossing~\cite{kuznetsov2026phase}. Full $AGI^{*}=E_{\mathrm{endo}}\land C_{\mathrm{non\text{-}emb}}(H)\land\cdots$ requires conjunction over $\tau_{AGI}$; this paper restricts to a \emph{proto-AGI} operational subset excluding $N_H$ and $D$ closure.

\textbf{Intrinsic motivation.} Curiosity and empowerment RL optimize scalar intrinsic signals~\cite{schmidhuber2010curiosity,pathak2017curiosity}. EIA targets multi-loop genesis with abstention, governor clearance, and causal traceability~\cite{kuznetsov2026stable}. The proto-AGI ensemble tests whether \emph{any} registered substrate sustains a four-parameter conjunction, not whether any single scalar reward suffices.

\textbf{Language-agent architectures.} CoALA surveys memory, planning, and grounding for language agents but notes weak causal evaluation of ``agency'' claims~\cite{sumers2024cala}. Sci-flow's ATT suite and proof-protocol ledger reject declaration-only evidence (F-DECL, F-NARR)~\cite{kuznetsov2026proof}.

\textbf{Analog computation in neuroscience.} Miller-lab theory proposes that cognition and consciousness arise from spatiotemporal \emph{analog} traveling-wave computation, with slow alpha/beta bands controlling fast gamma~\cite{miller2026analog}. EIA maps this \emph{hypothetically} to multi-band $\mathrm{OMEGA}_t$ and WoE carrier sweeps---computational explore adjunct, not biological proof~\cite{kuznetsov2026omega}.

\textbf{Goal genesis vs.\ selection; endogeneity $\neq$ autonomy.} Internal goal \emph{selection} from a human-designed menu is $AI_1$, not $PS$. Endogenous \emph{genesis} requires $G_{t+1}$ to arise from $(S_t,W_t,M_t,G_t)$ when $X_t^{\mathrm{trigger}}=0$. $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$ concerns \emph{causal origin}, not unconstrained action, tool use, or governor absence~\cite{kuznetsov2026star}. Description, simulation, and declaration of agency do not establish endogeneity.

\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{No AGI$^{*}$ claim.} $N_H$, $D$, and sustained $\tau_{AGI}$ conjunction are out of scope. Vendor substrates (Neuraxon, Graphitti) remain Tier C with \texttt{e\_endo\_support=none} for D1 ledger.
\item \textbf{C2 ceiling.} Ensemble peaks, partial G2 E01, and OMEGA decorrelation do \textbf{not} raise C-level.
\item \textbf{Thresholds TBD.} $\theta_E,\theta_\Omega,\theta_P,\theta_R,\Delta T$ are not pre-registered in this paper.
\item \textbf{Ensemble not batched.} T-PROTO-01 $\Phi_i$ batch is proposed, not executed here.
\item \textbf{OMEGA decorative (confirmed).} F-OMEGA-DECOR aggregate confirms high OMEGA can coexist with unchanged genesis fingerprint---OMEGA is explore adjunct, not Tier-A proof.
\item \textbf{Computational Hz only.} 20/30/42/70\,Hz are sweep parameters, not biological certificates.
\item \textbf{Partial domains.} G2 E01 covers 8/20 worlds $\times$ 1/3 domains; EUIR/E01 closure deferred.
\item \textbf{Non-final status.} This paper reports a \emph{research horizon} with scoped partial evidence; conclusions may be revised by falsifiers or expanded harnesses.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We operationalized a twelve-member proto-AGI ensemble and Max consensus framework over $(E,\mathrm{OMEGA},P,R)$ under explicit C2 ceiling and \texttt{claim\_allowed=false} discipline. The theory horizon (\S\ref{sec:theory-horizon}) states $AGI^{*}=E_{\mathrm{endo}}\land C_{\mathrm{non\text{-}emb}}(H)$ and $\tau_{AGI}$ as conjecture only; the NAMM parallel strand (\S\ref{sec:namm-lineage}) maps $N_H$ instrumentation to verification-first non-anthropic mathematics as open research, not proof. Passive controls anchor the non-AGI baseline; proactive candidates require causal $do(Z)$ evidence (CF-4 0.95/0.06/0.00; ledger \texttt{e\_endo\_support=partial}). D2 evidence---ATT-R 1.0/0.0, DSR 0.822--0.912, EOI drift 0.755---and G2 E01 8/20 worlds map onto the 3D cube without authorizing $AGI^{*}$. OMEGA$_t$ and $\Phi_{\max}$ are negative or decorative order parameters (F-OMEGA-DECOR confirmed). Closing ensemble batch, pre-registering thresholds, expanding G2 E01, and instrumenting $N_H$ remain open.
