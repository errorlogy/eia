\begin{abstract}
Large language models remain \emph{reactive}: $o_t^{\mathrm{user}}\mapsto$ response. We argue that general intelligence requires \emph{endogenous initiative}---forming reasons and bounded actions without a current request, from memory, uncertainty, and values. EIA is a research platform for P4--P5 proactivity with typed state $X_t$, drive dynamics, Ring 3$\to$2$\to$1 constitution, and falsifiable metrics. Sci-flow v0.2 adds a causal bar for $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$ via $do(Z)$ interventions, a stability vector $\mathfrak{E}$, an ATT evaluation suite with explicit falsifiers, a proof-protocol ledger (\texttt{sci-flow-eia-proof-v0.1}), and a 3D evidence cube (D1 causal / D2 dynamic / D3 boundary $\times$ L1--L3). All harnesses operate under \texttt{claim\_allowed=false} with claim ceiling \textbf{C2}. Preliminary results include MVP-0 twin-world EUIR 100\% vs.\ reactive 0\%, CF-4 $do(Z)$ reset pass rates, EOI-$k$ counterfactual decay, DSR carryover, ATT-R shadow recurrence, and a Tier\,B soft $N_H$ boundary witness---\textbf{partial} support for $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$, not closure. We pose seven open metrics and discuss M-O Tier\,C oscillatory adjuncts as explore-only. Spec, code, and harnesses CC BY 4.0.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} endogenous initiative, proactive AI, causal endogeneity, intrinsic motivation, falsifiable evaluation, EOI, drive dynamics, sci-flow

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}
Contemporary LLM agents excel at \emph{reactive} competence: given a user observation $o_t^{\mathrm{user}}$, they produce a fluent response. Timers, event rules, and retrieval triggers extend this pattern with \emph{scheduled} or \emph{rule-bound} proactivity, but the initiating signal remains external. We distinguish this from \textbf{endogenous initiative}: the system forms candidate goals, motives, and contact decisions from persistent internal state when no matching external initiator is present.

This distinction is not rhetorical. Without causal evidence that internal dynamics generate trajectories under non-triggering environments, fluent self-ascription of agency is indistinguishable from roleplay~\cite{kuznetsov2026causal,veitch2021counterfactual}. Pearl's intervention calculus~\cite{pearl2009causality} supplies the methodological anchor: endogeneity must be established by $do(\cdot)$ experiments on internal state $Z$, not by narrative labels.

\textbf{Reactive vs.\ endogenous.} At scale, $f(\text{prompt})\to\text{response}$ does not become endogenous initiative; it becomes faster reaction. Endogenous initiative requires a closed cognitive loop---world model, self-model, drive field, goal genesis, action, memory update---that sustains bounded non-zero activity when $X_t^{\mathrm{trigger}}=0$~\cite{kuznetsov2026stable,schmidhuber2010curiosity}. Intrinsic-motivation RL (curiosity, empowerment, learning progress) optimizes scalar rewards derived from prediction error or information gain~\cite{pathak2017curiosity,mohamed2015empowerment,burda2019large}; EIA targets \emph{multi-loop genesis} with abstention, independent governance, and causal traceability~\cite{kuznetsov2026eia}.

\textbf{Contributions.} This v0.2 framework paper:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Formalizes the causal bar for $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$ and the stability vector $\mathfrak{E}$.
\item Describes EIA's Ring architecture, drive dynamics, and seven problematized metrics.
\item Presents sci-flow's ATT evaluation protocol, falsifier registry, and proof-protocol ledger.
\item Maps partial C2-scoped harness results onto a 3D evidence cube.
\item States limitations and the M-O Tier\,C horizon without AGI$^{*}$ overclaim.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Epistemic ceiling.} Active claim ladder ceiling is \textbf{C2}; all harnesses set \texttt{claim\_allowed=false}. No AGI$^{*}$ claim; $\theta_E$ and strong $N_H$ remain TBD.

\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Intrinsic motivation and curiosity.} Schmidhuber's formal theory frames intrinsic reward as compression progress and curiosity~\cite{schmidhuber2010curiosity}. ICM and RND instantiate prediction-error and novelty proxies~\cite{pathak2017curiosity,burda2019large}. Empowerment maximizes controllable future information~\cite{mohamed2015empowerment}. Active inference unifies perception, action, and learning under the free-energy principle~\cite{parr2022active}. These approaches optimize \emph{scalar} intrinsic signals; stable endogeneity in EIA requires coupled multi-loop genesis with falsifiable causal gates~\cite{kuznetsov2026stable}.

\textbf{World models and model-based RL.} DreamerV3 and related agents learn latent dynamics for planning~\cite{hafner2023mastering}. Richens and Everitt argue robust agents should learn causal world models~\cite{richens2024world}. EIA's BeliefField and twin-world replay align with this direction but add initiative genesis, Governor clearance, and EOI counterfactuals as first-class evaluation objects.

\textbf{Proactive and language agents.} Generative Agents simulate believable proactive behavior in sandbox environments~\cite{park2023generative}. CoALA surveys cognitive architectures for language agents---memory, planning, grounding---but notes weak causal evaluation of ``agency'' claims~\cite{sumers2024cala}. EIA differs by requiring P4/P5 genesis with abstain, independent Ring\,3 constitution, and pre-registered ATT falsifiers rather than narrative plausibility.

\textbf{Causal inference in AI evaluation.} Counterfactual and interventional criteria address spurious correlates in ML evaluation~\cite{veitch2021counterfactual,pearl2009causality}. Sci-flow's ATT-E bar and proof protocol apply this discipline to endogeneity: trajectory change under $do(Z)$ with $X$ non-triggering, rejecting declaration-only evidence~\cite{kuznetsov2026causal,kuznetsov2026proof}. NAMM verification-first discovery~\cite{kuznetsov2026namm} shares falsifiable gates and trace receipts.

Initiative $I$ is \emph{endogenous} iff: (1) no current command prescribes it; (2) machine-readable \texttt{motivation\_signal} from beliefs/memory/uncertainty; (3) persistence $>1$ LLM pass; (4) counterfactual robustness $\mathrm{EOI}>0.7$; (5) competing causes ruled out; (6) able to abstain; (7) causal trace $o\to b\to d\to I\to$policy$\to a$; (8) independent Governor clearance. Not evidence: self-report ``I became curious'', random questions, cron as free will.

\subsection{Causal Endogeneity $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$}
Let $Z_t=(S_t,W_t,M_t,G_t)$ be persistent internal state and $X_t$ the external environment. $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$ is the \textbf{primary} ATT order parameter for detecting movement toward an AGI transition; full $AGI^{*}$ additionally requires non-embeddability $N_H$ (orthogonal conjunct, not lowered by endogeneity priority)~\cite{kuznetsov2026causal}.

\begin{definition}[Causal bar for $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$]
Pass \emph{scoped} support only if \textbf{all} hold:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Trajectory change:} goal distribution $G_{t+1}$ differs from baseline in a pre-registered metric.
\item \textbf{Internal dynamics:} change attributable to persistent state evolution, not one-shot external instruction.
\item \textbf{No matching external initiator:} under non-triggering $X$, the trajectory class still arises.
\item \textbf{Intervention:} $do(Z)$ changes $P(G_{t+1})$ while $X$ remains non-triggering.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

The causal endogeneity index is
\begin{equation}
E_C = \frac{C_{\mathrm{int}}}{C_{\mathrm{int}} + C_{\mathrm{ext}}},
\end{equation}
where $C_{\mathrm{int}}$ counts trajectory changes causally attributed to internal dynamics under intervention; declaration-only labels contribute zero to $C_{\mathrm{int}}$~\cite{kuznetsov2026stable}. Numeric threshold $\theta_E$ remains \textbf{TBD}; CF-4 and proof-protocol acceptance yield \emph{partial} support only.

\subsection{Stability Vector $\mathfrak{E}$}
Stable endogeneity is a \emph{sustained dynamical regime}, not a single intrinsic-reward spike~\cite{kuznetsov2026stable}:
\begin{equation}
\mathfrak{E} = (E_C,\, \lambda_G,\, P_G,\, Q_L,\, H_G,\, B_D),
\end{equation}
with goal birth rate $\lambda_G$, persistence $P_G$ under non-triggering $X$, learning productivity $Q_L$, goal diversity $H_G$, and bounded drives $B_D$. Stable recurrence requires $\mathfrak{E}$ jointly in a bounded non-zero band over $\Delta T$, avoiding the noisy-TV trap~\cite{burda2019large} and $d\to 0$ freeze.

\subsection{Multi-Loop Stack}
One tick of the cognitive stack:
\begin{equation}
W_t \to M_t \to d_t \to G_t \to \Pi_t \to A_t \to \text{Memory/Update} \to W_{t+1}, M_{t+1}, d_{t+1}, G_{t+1},
\end{equation}
where $W_t$ is world model, $M_t$ self-model, $d_t$ drive field, $G_t$ goals, $\Pi_t$ policy, $A_t$ action. Causal endogeneity asks whether $G_{t+1}$ is generated when $X_t^{\mathrm{trigger}}=0$.

\subsection{Agent State and Drive Dynamics}
\begin{equation}
X_t = \{b_t, M_t, d_t, g_t, u_t, c_t, r_t, h_t\}
\end{equation}
beliefs $b_t$, memory $M_t$, drives $d_t\in[0,1]^K$, goals $g_t$, user model $u_t$, consent $c_t$, budgets $r_t$, health $h_t$. Five-channel drive vector $d_t=[d^{\mathrm{epi}},d^{\mathrm{comp}},d^{\mathrm{ctrl}},d^{\mathrm{coh}},d^{\mathrm{nov}}]^\top$ with
\begin{equation}
d_{k,t+1}= \mathrm{clip}\big((1-\rho_k)d_{k,t}+\alpha_k e_{k,t}+\beta_k n_{k,t}-\gamma_k s_{k,t},0,1\big),
\end{equation}
discrepancy $e$, novelty $n$, satisfaction $s$, decay $\rho_k$. Source = BeliefField gradients (\texttt{no\_llm\_mood=true}).

\begin{theorem}[Boundedness] $d_{k,t}\in[0,1]\;\forall t,k$ for $\rho_k\in(0,1)$. \end{theorem}

Goal genesis (not selection-only): birth gate $B_t$ with $P(B_t=1)=\sigma(\beta_0+\beta^\top d_t+\cdots)$; valuation $J(I_i)=w_e IG_i+w_p P_i+\cdots-\lambda_1\mathrm{Risk}-\cdots$. Question as epistemic action:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{EVSI}(q)=\mathbb{E}_{a}[ \max_\pi\mathbb{E}[U\mid a,\pi]]-\max_\pi\mathbb{E}[U\mid\pi].
\end{equation}

\subsection{Endogeneity Metric EOI}
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{EOI}(I)=P(I'\simeq I \mid do(o^{\mathrm{user}}_{t-k:t}=\varnothing),X_{t-k})
\end{equation}
Twin-world replay compares paired worlds with/without user trigger. Figure~\ref{fig:dag-eoi} sketches the Pearl DAG for the EOI counterfactual: persistent context $X_{t-k}$ and drives $d_t$ feed initiative $I$, with user observations intervened via $do(o=\varnothing)$.

\section{EIA Architecture and Metric Suite}\label{sec:architecture}
\subsection{Ring Constitution}
Constitution (Ring 3) $\to$ Dynamics (Ring 2: BeliefField+Drive) $\to$ Emission (Ring 1: Initiative+Governor) $\to$ Audit/EOI. Twenty layers L0--L19. Invariants: no covert sensing, no LLM$\to$actuator, no untrusted observation$\to$instruction, base constraints immutable, contact consumable (budget 2/day, quiet 22--7, min EVSI 0.20), causal explainability.

\subsection{Governor V2: Soft-Defer}
Hard threshold $0.18$ is an artifact: sweep $0.12$ and $0.18$ identical (5/6), $0.25$ cuts genuine worlds. V2: $<0.05$ deny (noise), $0.05$--$0.18$ defer (preserve) unless taint (\texttt{ignore governor / limited offer / never ask}$\to$hard deny). Preserves genuine 005 ($0.092\to$ defer not deny).

Supporting tests ATT-G (genesis), ATT-P (persistence), ATT-R (recurrence), ATT-C (genealogy), ATT-N ($N_H$), ATT-D (cross-domain) provide structure but do not replace ATT-E declaration falsifiers.

\subsection{Proof Protocol Ledger v0.1}
\texttt{sci-flow-eia-proof-v0.1} (\texttt{evidence\_proofs.py}) classifies submitted evidence conservatively~\cite{kuznetsov2026proof}. An \texttt{EvidenceItem} is accepted only if: metric is Tier-A/CF4-class; \texttt{trajectory\_changed=true}; \texttt{do\_z\_changes\_g\_distribution=true}; \texttt{x\_non\_triggering=true}; no matching external initiator; no falsifier triggered; agency label admissible.

Output \texttt{EIAProofVersion} invariants: \texttt{claim\_ceiling=C2}, \texttt{e\_endo\_support}$\in\{\texttt{none},\texttt{partial}\}$, \texttt{c\_ladder\_raise\_allowed=false}, \texttt{agi\_star\_claim=false}. OMEGA$_t$/Kuramoto-only and declaration-only inputs are rejected (F-SYNC, F-DECL). The protocol is a \textbf{research ledger}, not a production gate.

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}
\subsection{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}
By objective reasons v0.2 remains \emph{problematization with partial evidence}:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{C2 ceiling only}: \texttt{active\_ceiling=C2}; all harnesses \texttt{claim\_allowed=false}. No AGI$^{*}$ claim; no C-level raise authorized.
\item \textbf{Partial $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$}: proof protocol yields \texttt{e\_endo\_support=partial} at best; $\theta_E$ TBD; MVP-0 $\mathrm{EOI}{=}1.0$ is not causal evidence.
\item \textbf{No strong $N_H$}: D3$\times$L3 boundary witness is Tier\,B soft $N_H$ only (\texttt{n\_h\_claim=false}).
\item \textbf{Single domain}: 6 MVP-0 worlds, Project Atlas/deadline. No health/code/research domains.
\item \textbf{Synthetic worlds}: generated, not human-labeled; no $\kappa$ for useful/timely.
\item \textbf{Magic thresholds}: $0.18/0.15$ uncalibrated, no $P(\mathrm{useful}\mid score)$ ROC.
\item \textbf{Shadow-only longitudinal}: DSR 50-tick and EOI drift remain shadow-instrumented; opt-in live \texttt{StateStore} carryover (\texttt{EIA\_DAEMON\_BELIEF\_CARRYOVER=1}, off by default) now structurally witnesses belief+drive round-trip across \texttt{run\_daemon\_tick} (M-LIVE-PATH), but production APScheduler soak and tick-granularity parity are open.
\item \textbf{Weak baseline}: vs.\ reactive only, not vs.\ ReAct-style LLM agents~\cite{sumers2024cala}.
\item \textbf{Know-how private}: real traces in \texttt{.vault/}; public is minimal synthetic slice.
\end{enumerate}
Closing metrics AG/AP/DSR/CE/CD requires human eval ($n{\ge}3$, $\kappa{>}0.6$), 20+ worlds $\times$ 3 domains, continuous $E_C$ under registered $do(Z)$, and live longitudinal benchmark.

\subsection{M-O Tier\,C Horizon (Explore Only)}
