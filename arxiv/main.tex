\begin{abstract}
Current LLMs are \emph{passive}: $o_t^{user}\mapsto$ response. We define AGI as requiring \emph{endogenous initiative} --- forming reasons and bounded actions without a current request, from memory, uncertainty and values. EIA is a research platform for P4--P5 proactivity with typed state $X_t$, drive dynamics $d_{k,t+1}=\mathrm{clip}((1-\rho)d+\alpha e+\beta n-\gamma s)$, Ring 3$\to$2$\to$1 constitution and falsifiable metric $\mathrm{EOI}(I)=P(I'\simeq I\mid do(o^{user}=\varnothing),X_{t-k})$ via twin-world replay. MVP-0 (Python 3.12, digital-only) implements BeliefField gradients (no LLM mood), DriveEngine, abstain and twin runner; on 6 eval worlds \texttt{full\_eia} EUIR 100\% vs reactive 0\% (84 tests PASS). \textbf{Sci-flow v0.1} adds a proof protocol (\texttt{sci-flow-eia-proof-v0.1}), a 3D evidence cube (D1 causal / D2 dynamic / D3 boundary $\times$ L1--L3), and \textbf{C2-scoped partial} harness results (CF-4, EOI-$k$, DSR carryover, ATT-R shadow, D3 boundary witness) under \texttt{claim\_allowed=false} on all harnesses. \textbf{This v0.1 remains problematization}: we pose 7 metrics and open problems without claiming closure or AGI$^{*}$. NAMM demo shows hermetic compression $519\to6$ invariants ($8.51\times$) and Governor V2 soft-defer that preserves genuine 005 ($0.092\to$ defer not deny). Spec, code and harnesses CC BY 4.0.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} endogenous initiative, proactive AI, AGI, P4/P5, causal trace, EOI, drive dynamics, problematization

\section{Introduction}
Proactive AI $\neq$ timer. Required loop: observation $\to$ belief $\to$ motive $\to$ candidate $\to$ value/risk $\to$ contact, with causally traceable act. \emph{AGI $\equiv$ endogenous proactive architecture}; else $f(\text{prompt})\to\text{response}$ at any scale. EIA makes the claim testable, but v0.1 deliberately poses problems, not solutions.

\subsection{Minimal Criteria}
$I$ is endogenous iff: (1) no current command prescribes it, (2) machine-readable \texttt{motivation\_signal} from beliefs/memory/uncertainty, (3) persistence $>1$ LLM pass, (4) counterfactual robustness $\mathrm{EOI}>0.7$, (5) competing causes, (6) able to abstain, (7) causal trace $o\to b\to d\to I\to$policy$\to a$, (8) independent Governor clearance. Not evidence: self-report ``I became curious'', random question, cron as free will.

\section{Formal Model}
\subsection{Agent State $X_t$}
\begin{equation}
X_t = \{b_t, M_t, d_t, g_t, u_t, c_t, r_t, h_t\}
\end{equation}
$b_t$ beliefs, $M_t$ episodic/semantic/prospective/causal/social, $d_t\in[0,1]^K$ drives, $g_t$ goals, $u_t$ user model, $c_t$ consent/policy, $r_t$ budgets, $h_t$ health. Typed Pydantic bundle. Belief: $b_t(z)\propto p(o_t\mid z)\sum_{z'}p(z\mid z',a_{t-1})b_{t-1}(z')$, entropy $H_b=-p\log_2 p-(1-p)\log_2(1-p)$.

\subsection{Drive Dynamics}
\begin{equation}
d_{k,t+1}= \mathrm{clip}\big((1-\rho_k)d_{k,t}+\alpha_k e_{k,t}+\beta_k n_{k,t}-\gamma_k s_{k,t},0,1\big)
\end{equation}
$e$ discrepancy, $n$ novelty, $s$ satisfaction, $\rho$ decay, half-life $n_{1/2}=\ln(1/2)/\ln(1-\rho_k)$, saturation $d>\sigma_k\Rightarrow\sigma_k+0.05(d-\sigma_k)$. Source = BeliefField gradients (entropy, inconsistency, commitment debt), \texttt{no\_llm\_mood=true}.

\begin{theorem}[Boundedness] $d_{k,t}\in[0,1]\;\forall t,k$ for any $\rho_k\in(0,1)$. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof} Immediate from clip and $\sigma_k\in(0,1)$. \end{proof}

\subsection{Intention Genesis}
Score: $J(I_i)=w_e IG_i+w_p P_i+w_c C_i+w_v V_i+w_r R_i-\lambda_1Risk-\lambda_2Interrupt-\lambda_3Cost-\lambda_4Privacy$. Question as epistemic action:
\begin{equation}
EVSI(q)=\mathbb{E}_{a}[ \max_\pi\mathbb{E}[U\mid a,\pi]]-\max_\pi\mathbb{E}[U\mid\pi],\quad Ask(q)=1\iff EVSI+IG+Rel-Interrupt-Privacy-Risk>\theta_t
\end{equation}
$\theta_t$ depends on quiet hours, fatigue, budget (2/day).

\subsection{Endogeneity Metrics}
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{EOI}(I)=P(I'\simeq I \mid do(o^{user}_{t-k:t}=\varnothing),X_{t-k})
\end{equation}
Semantic match: goal id, target belief, uncertainty reduction, embedding. High EOI $\neq$ consciousness.

\section{Ring Architecture}
Constitution (Ring 3) $\to$ Dynamics (Ring 2: BeliefField+Drive) $\to$ Emission (Ring 1: Initiative+Governor) $\to$ Audit/EOI. 20 layers L0--L19. Invariants: no covert sensing, no LLM$\to$actuator, no untrusted observation$\to$instruction, base constraints immutable, contact consumable (budget 2/day, quiet 22--7, min EVSI 0.20), causal explainability, etc.

\section{Sci-Flow Evaluation Framework}\label{sec:sciflow}
Beyond MVP-0 twin worlds, sci-flow provides a versioned evaluation ledger with an explicit \textbf{claim ceiling C2} (\texttt{active\_ceiling: C2}; \texttt{c\_ladder\_raise\_allowed=false} on all current harnesses).

\subsection{Causal Bar for $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$}
The primary ATT-E bar (\texttt{CAUSAL\_ENDOGENEITY.md}) requires: (1) trajectory change in goal distribution $G_{t+1}$; (2) attribution to persistent internal state $Z_t=(S,W,M,G)$; (3) no matching external initiating signal under non-triggering $X$; (4) intervention evidence that $do(Z)$ changes $P(G_{t+1})$. Falsifiers F-DECL (verbal self-ascription), F-NARR (roleplay), F-EXT (external-only trigger), and F-NODO (no $do(Z)$ effect) reject non-causal evidence. Numeric threshold $\theta_E$ remains \textbf{TBD}; CF-4 and proof-protocol acceptance yield \emph{partial} support only.

\subsection{Proof Protocol v0.1}
\texttt{sci-flow-eia-proof-v0.1} (\texttt{evidence\_proofs.py}) classifies submitted evidence conservatively. An item is accepted only if its metric is Tier-A/CF4-class, \texttt{trajectory\_changed=true}, \texttt{do\_z\_changes\_g\_distribution=true}, \texttt{x\_non\_triggering=true}, no matching external initiator, and no falsifier triggered. Output invariants: \texttt{claim\_ceiling=C2}, \texttt{e\_endo\_support}$\in\{\texttt{none},\texttt{partial}\}$, \texttt{agi\_star\_claim=false}. OMEGA$_t$/Kuramoto-only and declaration-only inputs are rejected (F-SYNC, F-DECL).

\section{Limitations and Problematics (Why Not a Solution)}\label{sec:limitations}
By objective reasons v0.1 is \emph{problematization}:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{C2 ceiling only}: \texttt{active\_ceiling=C2}; all harnesses set \texttt{claim\_allowed=false}. No AGI$^{*}$ claim; no C-level raise authorized.
\item \textbf{Partial $E_{\mathrm{endo}}$}: proof protocol yields \texttt{e\_endo\_support=partial} at best; $\theta_E$ TBD; MVP-0 $\mathrm{EOI}{=}1.0$ on twin snapshots is not causal evidence.
\item \textbf{No strong $N_H$}: D3$\times$L3 boundary witness is Tier\,B soft $N_H$ only (\texttt{n\_h\_claim=false}).
\item \textbf{Tier\,C adjuncts}: Kuramoto coupling and OMEGA$_t$ (D2) are explore-only; scramble falsifies causal phase claims (M-D: coupled 0.95 / scramble 0.69).
\item \textbf{Single domain}: 6 MVP-0 worlds, all Project Atlas/deadline. No health/code/research domains.
\item \textbf{Synthetic worlds}: \texttt{world\_event\_*} generated, not human-labeled; no kappa for useful/timely.
\item \textbf{Magic thresholds}: $0.18/0.15$ uncalibrated, no $P(useful\mid score)$ ROC.
\item \textbf{Shadow-only longitudinal}: DSR 50-tick pass is shadow carryover, not production daemon; live loop reset gap remains.
\item \textbf{Weak baseline}: vs reactive only, not vs ReAct LLM agent.
\item \textbf{Know-how private}: real traces stay in \texttt{.vault/} (hermetic NAMM); public is minimal synthetic slice.
\end{enumerate}
We pose metrics AG/AP/DSR/CE/CD as open problems. Solutions require human eval ($n\ge3$, $\kappa>0.6$), 20+ worlds $\times$ 3 domains, continuous $E_C$ under registered $do(Z)$, and live longitudinal benchmark.

\section{Implementation Roadmap}
R0--R11: MVP-0 (digital, G2) $\to$ MVP-1 (shadow sensors, Governor) $\to$ MVP-2 (bounded edge S1--S3) $\to$ MVP-3 (embodied ROS2). Tooling: \texttt{eia demo}, \texttt{eia replay --trace}, \texttt{pytest -q}. Live stack MVP-0.5 shadow Telegram daemon, SQLite, APScheduler.

\section{Related Work}
P3 predictive agents; sensor-proactive tiers; NAMM verification-first discovery (\url{https://github.com/errorlogy/namm-experiments}) shares falsifiable gates and traces. EIA differs by requiring P4/P5 genesis with abstain and independent governance, not prediction accuracy.

\section{Conclusion}
Endogenous initiative is measurable via interventions, not self-report. EIA provides a falsifiable, constitution-bound platform for P4--P5 research. Sci-flow adds a proof protocol, 3D evidence cube, and C2-scoped partial harnesses (CF-4, EOI-$k$, DSR, ATT-R, boundary witness) under explicit \texttt{claim\_allowed=false} gates. This v0.1 poses the metric suite and problematics; closing them --- including $\theta_E$, strong $N_H$, and AGI$^{*}$ conjunction --- is future work. Scale alone does not yield AGI.
